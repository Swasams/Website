Normal-Bad Sex

A series of playable podcasts about real people’s uncomfortable sexual experiences, uniting oral history with video games. Real interview audio sits beside small, abstract mini-games that highlight what the speaker felt, not what they did.

i.

The brief

Normal-bad sex is the category most of us have a story in and no vocabulary for. It’s the stories that are negative but not traumatic, the night that stays with you for a long time because you can’t quite name what went wrong.

Normal-Bad Sex is a collection of playable podcasts that gives that category a place to land. Each episode pairs a real interview, recorded with someone who has already sat with what happened, alongside a small set of mini-games that don’t dramatise what’s being said. The mechanics highlight the speaker’s emotion.

It was conceived and co-designed with Nyusha Iampolski and Sophie Chu at NYU Game Center, and funded by an NYU grant.

ii.

The work

One · Menu Design
A title that settles into a menu
Two · The Game Loop
Listening at the bar, playing between rounds

Between mini-games, the player sits at a bar with the speaker, or a jungle stand-in for the speaker, and listens. To keep the hands busy without pulling focus, I added a popcorn fidget to play with. It acts as a short pause between each minigame.

Three · Mini Games
Designed for the feeling, not the event

The mini-games are metaphorical. Each one is a small interaction that mirrors how the speaker felt at a given moment. A birthday box you unwrap, over and over, until the label finally reads something you didn’t ask for.

Four · Process
Interview to transcript to verb

Every episode starts as an interview. Nyusha records it and cleans the audio; we transcribe it together and edit the text down to the shape we want to tell. Then a second pass: we edit the audio to match the trimmed text, and start pulling verbs out of the transcript, moments where the speaker’s feeling should be emphasised.

Five · Episode 2 · Building Generator
A New York laid out by decade

Episode two is about aging. The interview is with a woman in her seventies recounting her sexual life across the decades, and the game is structured around that, each decade is laid out as a different road in New York City. To populate the streets, I built a procedural building generator: a system of variables that produces normal buildings across a stretch of road, with custom buildings dropped in at the key story beats.

Six · Episode 2 · Custom buildings & gameplay
The moments that aren’t procedural

The procedural buildings are the background. The custom ones are the story. Passing through the road auto triggers some parts of the story with characters and custom buildings triggering the other related stories.
