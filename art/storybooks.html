i.

The brief

This is a compilation of the illustrated books and comics I’ve worked on, these are mostly children’s picture books adapted from Munshi Premchand’s short stories while I interned at Pratham Books, Bangalore for their Storyweaver platform. Storyweaver is an open-storytelling platform from Pratham Books.

They share a sensibility: small, human stories, often about the people we overlook, the old, the poor, the unheard.
